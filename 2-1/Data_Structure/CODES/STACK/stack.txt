REVERSE POLISH NOTATION 
- also known as postfix NOTATION

Infix needs parentheses and precedence rules.
 In 3 + 4 * 5, you must know that * goes first.
In postfix, the order is already built in, so no parentheses and no precedence rules are needed. That makes it easy for a computer to evaluate with a stack.

Example: 3 + 4 * 5 becomes 3 4 5 * +.
